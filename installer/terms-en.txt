1. WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS

Todo Downloader is a general-purpose download manager. It retrieves files
over HTTP, from public links on file-hosting services, from the BitTorrent
network, and — by driving separate third-party programs — from video and
gallery websites.

It is a tool. Like a web browser, an FTP client or a command-line downloader,
it performs the network requests that the person operating it asks it to
perform. It has no opinion about, and no knowledge of, the content it moves.

7. BITTORRENT IN PARTICULAR

While a torrent is active, this program does not only download: it also
UPLOADS data to other participants in the swarm. That is how the protocol
works and it cannot be otherwise while participating.

This means that (i) your IP address is visible to every other participant and
to anyone monitoring the swarm, and (ii) depending on the material, you may
be making it available to the public, which in many jurisdictions is a
distinct and more serious act than downloading it. Consider this carefully
before using the Torrent tab.
