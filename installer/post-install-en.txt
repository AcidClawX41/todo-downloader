Todo Downloader
===============

BEFORE YOU START

Direct links, MEGA, file hosts and BitTorrent work straight away: those
engines are compiled into the application itself.

For video and gallery sites you need two helpers, and the application
downloads them for you from Settings:

  - yt-dlp      video pages (YouTube, TikTok, Bilibili, 1000+ sites)
  - gallery-dl  galleries and profiles
  - ffmpeg      merges video and audio, needed for anything above 720p
                on YouTube and for Bilibili at all

They are not bundled on purpose: yt-dlp in particular is updated constantly
because the sites it supports keep changing, and a copy frozen inside this
installer would stop working on YouTube within weeks.

SESSIONS

Instagram, Weibo, Douyin, private profiles and age-restricted content need
your browser session. Settings -> Browser cookies, or point the application
at a cookies.txt exported with a browser extension. Without a session, only
direct links and public YouTube are reliable.

DOUYIN AND THREADS PROFILES

No extractor lists them, so they are read from inside your own browser. The
Capture tab gives you a script; installing it as a userscript (Tampermonkey
or Violentmonkey) is the better route, and on Threads it is the recommended
one. What it captures comes back into the Profile tab, with previews, so you
pick what to download. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the application on
127.0.0.1.

DISCOVER ARTISTS

The Artists tab turns a character name into the profiles that draw them. It
builds no index of its own: it reads the "source" field of booru posts, which
points back at the artist's original post on X, Pixiv, Patreon or Fanbox. Pick
one and it goes to the queue or opens with previews.

It only finds what somebody attributed. An artist nobody uploads to a booru is
invisible there, and no setting changes that.

MAGNET LINKS

If you want magnet: links to open in Todo Downloader, enable it from
Settings. It is not done during installation because it changes a protocol
association on your system, and that should be your decision, not the
installer's.

WHAT THIS UNINSTALLER WILL NOT DELETE

Your downloads and your settings. Settings live in your user profile and are
left alone, so reinstalling keeps your configuration.

The helper engines are not deleted either. They live in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TodoDownloader\bin and are shared with any portable copy of
Todo Downloader, so removing them here would break that copy. Delete that
folder by hand if you want the disk space back.

ANIMATED GIFS

The "tips" folder next to the executable can hold .gif files shown in the
support tab. It ships empty with only a README: those GIFs would be
third-party material and are not distributed with the application.
